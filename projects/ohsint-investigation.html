01

Approach and tools

Used Windows, PowerShell and ExifTool to inspect a challenge image, identify XMP-exif GPS-related metadata and correlate public information.

02

Evidence and validation

Completed all seven TryHackMe questions with coaching. A Wi-Fi SSID observation was supplied by the coach and was not independently verified.

03

Result and limitations

Documented investigative limitations and proposed defensive controls for image-publishing workflows. Those controls have not been deployed or tested.

04

Lessons learned

Distinguish observed evidence from inference; inspect EXIF and XMP; avoid overstating verification.
