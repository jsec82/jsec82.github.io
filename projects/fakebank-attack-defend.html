01

Approach and tools

Inspected the simulated banking application in Firefox, used Dirb for authorized directory enumeration, discovered a staff-oriented deposit interface and completed the prescribed lab action.

02

Evidence and validation

TryHackMe accepted the offensive lab answer. Defensive Security Intro was completed previously, as reported by the learner; its individual tasks were not captured here.

03

Result and limitations

Documented the observed lab outcome and proposed defensive logging, server-side authorization and transaction validation controls. No defensive controls were implemented or verified.

04

Lessons learned

Unlinked web paths are not security boundaries. Authenticate users, authorize every privileged action server-side, and verify denied transactions against reliable audit records and account balances.
