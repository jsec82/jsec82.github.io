MY STORY

Curious about how security controls work — and whether they actually work.

I am developing my Security Engineering skills with an emphasis on the Microsoft ecosystem: device management, identity and access, endpoint security and investigation. I use structured learning, authorized lab environments and case studies to connect concepts to practical results.

The purpose of this website is straightforward: help a recruiter or engineering manager understand my hands-on development without having to browse a large technical repository. The documentation says what I did, what I observed, and where verification is incomplete.
